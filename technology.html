Three Pillars of Innovation

5G & IoT Connectivity

From 5G NR and LTE Cat-4 to Cat-M and NB-IoT, our devices leverage the right connectivity for every use case — balancing speed, coverage, and power efficiency. eSIM technology eliminates the need for physical SIM cards, enabling instant global activation.

Edge & Cloud AI

On-device AI models enable real-time behavior recognition, voice processing, and smart power management. Cloud AI delivers meeting summaries, health insights, digital twin synchronization, and natural language interaction.

End-to-end internal development

From chip-level hardware design and embedded firmware to mobile apps, cloud infrastructure, and manufacturing — everything is developed and controlled in-house. This full-stack ownership enables deep cross-layer optimization, faster iteration, and uncompromising quality at every stage.

These three pillars work together to form the core of our products, driving innovation and continuous improvement. They empower us to deliver smarter, more reliable, and scalable connected experiences for every future scenario.
